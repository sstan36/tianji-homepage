1. Who we are

This Policy applies to Michael Tan and the TianJi iOS application, including our use of service providers named in Section 8 (and Apple for the App Store and platform services). Contact details are in Section 16 at the end of this page.

2. Scope

The App lets you create and manage BaZi charts, with online support where a feature needs it, and optional AI-assisted consultant readings. This Policy covers: information processed on your device; information processed through our online services; and information you voluntarily email when you contact support. It does not govern websites or services operated by third parties that we do not control.

3. Categories of information

We describe data in plain language consistent with how Apple and many laws refer to personal information or personal data. Information you enter to generate a chart (such as birth date, birth time, timezone, and gender) is personal information because it identifies or relates to a person. We use it only as described here.

3.1 What you provide (chart inputs)

When you create or edit a chart, you may enter an optional label or name; date of birth, time of birth, timezone, and gender (needed for calculation); and notes stored locally with the chart. Avoid entering more sensitive data than you need; you are responsible for accuracy.

3.2 On your device

The App stores charts and related reading content, purchase and entitlement status, and preferences (for example language, disclaimer acceptance, and whether you allow AI reading data sharing) on your device. Unless sent to our servers or exported by you, we cannot access content stored only on your device.

3.3 On our servers

When you use features that rely on our online service (for example generating a chart, syncing purchases, or an AI-assisted reading), the App sends the information needed for that request. This may include a pseudonymous account ID (we do not use email/password accounts), basic app and device information needed to provide the service, chart inputs and/or structured chart results for calculation features, and for optional AI-assisted readings the reading text described in Section 6.

3.4 Technical data from online use

When you use online features, our service providers may process ordinary technical metadata needed to deliver the service (for example IP address, timestamps, and operational logs). We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services in the current App. We do not use cookies or mobile ad IDs for advertising or profiling across apps.

4. Support diagnostics and email

You may use Settings → Contact support (when present) to open an email to us. Nothing is sent automatically. You decide whether to tap send. We do not collect your email as an App account field; we only see an address if your mail app includes it when you send.

  • What the email may include (by design): device and app details for troubleshooting (for example device model, iOS version, app version, locale); a short excerpt of a diagnostic log from your device for recent errors.
  • What belongs out of the log: We intend not to include raw chart content, birth dates or times, or similar chart-identifying detail in this log. Entries should be short lines that help us debug without unnecessary personal detail.
  • If you need to share sensitive chart data to resolve a bug, do that only if you choose to type or attach it in the email. We will use only what you send to answer your support request, subject to normal support and legal retention.

5. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Generate, display, and store BaZi and related charts and readings
  • Provide optional AI-assisted consultant readings after you give permission in the App (see Section 6)
  • Manage purchases and entitlements
  • Operate, secure, and improve the service
  • Respond to support requests you send
  • Comply with law and our Terms of Use

Chart and AI-assisted reading output is for personal reflection only. It is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice; see the Terms of Use and Disclaimer in the App.

6. Generative AI and readings

Some consultant insights use a third-party AI service. When you use those features, we may share compressed reading text (and for some Qi Men insights, your selected question) with OpenAI so it can rewrite that text into a readable reading returned to the App.

We do not send your birth date or birth time, raw chart files, or an account email address to OpenAI for those features.

Before the first share, the App explains what is sent, that a third-party AI provider (OpenAI, as named here) will receive it, and asks for your permission. You can turn this off anytime in Settings under AI reading data sharing. If you turn it off, you will be asked again before the next AI-assisted reading.

AI output can be inaccurate. The App does not offer free-form AI chat.

Contact us if you need jurisdiction-specific information.

9. Retention

Data stored on your device stays on the device until you delete charts or app data in the App, uninstall the App, or erase the device. We cannot delete local files from our side.

Data stored on the server is kept only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal duties, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Support emails may be kept for a reasonable period to finish the thread and for internal quality, unless law requires longer retention.

10. Security

We use measures appropriate to the risk to protect personal data in transit and at rest, and to limit access to our systems. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate (including Google and, for AI-assisted readings, OpenAI). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (for example standard contractual clauses).

13. Your rights and choices

AI reading data sharing: Turn this off in Settings to withdraw permission for AI-assisted readings.

Data stored on your device: Delete charts or reset data in the App, or uninstall. That removes local copies from your device; it does not, by itself, erase server records that may still exist for your installation.

Data stored on the server: Email tianjisupport@gmail.com from an address you can receive mail at. Describe your request (delete my records stored on the server, access, etc.). Because accounts are anonymous (no email login), we may ask for reasonable information (for example your support email thread, approximate purchase date, or other non-sensitive cues) to match your request to the right records. Where deletion is possible, we will delete or anonymise relevant server data we control, subject to legal retention needs.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights (access, correction, export, object, restrict processing, withdraw consent where applicable). On iOS you can uninstall the App and use system privacy and network settings Apple provides. We respond within the time applicable law requires where that applies.

We do not sell personal information and do not use behavioural advertising across contexts as described in those laws for our App. For requests, contact tianjisupport@gmail.com. We do not discriminate for exercising these rights. Categories of information are described above; purposes in Section 5.
